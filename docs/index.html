About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, with a focus on software engineering, data engineering, and applied machine learning. My professional and academic work centers on building systems that hold up under real use — from the query behind a dashboard to the model behind a decision. I enjoy working at the intersection of backend engineering and ML: optimizing the systems data runs through, and building models that turn that data into something usable. I have hands-on experience with SQL optimization, data modeling, ETL pipelines, and deep learning, and I'm especially interested in how these come together to build reliable, production-grade software — from enterprise dashboards to applications people use directly. I'm always open to software engineering opportunities, collaborations, and conversations about building systems that work at scale.
